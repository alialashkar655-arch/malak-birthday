# Personalization Guide: How to Add Your Memories for Malak ❤️

This guide explains how you can personalize every part of the website with your real photos, favorite memories, videos, and love notes without writing any code.

---

## 🔑 Accessing the Admin CMS

1. Open the website: [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173)
2. Scroll to the bottom footer and click **"Admin & CMS Settings"** (or click the small **lock icon 🔒** in the top-right navbar).
3. Enter your admin password:
   - Default: `malak2026`
4. Click **"Unlock Admin Dashboard"**.

---

## 📸 Step-by-Step Personalization

### 1. Opening Screen Photo (Hero Section)
- In Admin CMS, go to the **"General & Hero"** tab.
- Look for **"Hero Photo URL or Upload"**.
- Click **"Upload"** and choose your favorite picture of you two together.
- Edit your title or intro message if you want custom wording.
- Click **"Save Settings"**.

### 2. "My Favorite Picture of You" Spotlight
- Go to the **"Favorite Photo"** tab.
- Click **"Upload Photo"** and select your favorite portrait of Malak.
- In **"Why I Love This Picture (Caption)"**, write down why you love this specific photo (e.g. what she was laughing about, where you were).
- Add a handwritten note (e.g. *"My favorite view in every lifetime. ♡"*).
- Add the date and location.
- Click **"Save Spotlight"**.

### 3. "Our Story" Timeline Memories
- Go to the **"Our Story Timeline"** tab.
- You will see the 7 curated milestone cards:
  1. *When We First Met*
  2. *Our First Picture Together*
  3. *Our Funniest Memory*
  4. *Our Favorite Date*
  5. *A Moment I Never Want to Forget*
  6. *Our Most Recent Memory*
  7. *A Future Memory We Have Yet to Make*
- Hover over each photo box and click **"Change Photo"** to upload your real picture or video.
- Edit the date and personal caption for each memory.

### 4. Photo & Video Gallery (Pinterest Board)
- Go to the **"Media Library"** tab.
- Click **"Upload New Media"** to upload images or video clips directly from your computer/phone.
- Assign each item to a category (*Our Pictures*, *Her Pictures*, *Dates*, *Random Moments*, *Videos*).
- You can add captions and dates.

### 5. "A Little Book of Us" (Digital Scrapbook Keepsake)
- Go to the **"Keepsake Book"** tab.
- Customize the album cover photo and title.
- Customize the secret page quote (*"Every little moment led me to you"*).
- Upload photos and captions for the album pages.
- Visitors can turn pages like a real printed album or click **"Print Keepsake / PDF"** to save it as a PDF!

### 6. Love Letters & "Open When..." Notes
- Go to the **"Love Letters"** tab.
- Personalize the main letter (*"Things I Don't Say Enough"*).
- Fill in the 5 "Open When..." notes:
  - *Open when you miss me*
  - *Open when you need a smile*
  - *Open when you're having a bad day*
  - *Open when you want to remember us*
  - *Open when you need a reminder of how loved you are*
- Click **"Save Letters"**.

### 7. Previewing the Birthday Celebration (November 20, 2026)
- The site automatically counts down to **November 20, 2026** using the **Africa/Cairo** timezone.
- To preview what the website looks like when her birthday arrives:
  - Inside the Admin Header, click **"Test Birthday Mode 🎂"**.
  - Or on the main countdown card, click **"Preview Birthday Reveal 🎂"**.
  - Confetti explodes, the countdown transforms into a celebration greeting, and your birthday letter and video are revealed!

### 8. Seeing Malak's Date Responses
- When Malak clicks *"Yes 💗"*, *"Absolutely!! 🥹"*, or *"HELL YEAH!! ❤️🔥"* on the date invitation and fills out the 7-step date questionnaire:
  1. Her complete choices (date type, flower color, flower types, food cravings, timing, activities, and personal note) are instantly sent and saved.
  2. Open the Admin Panel and click **"Date Submissions"** to read her answers and plan the surprise!

---

## 💾 Saving Backups
- In the Admin Panel under **"Backup & Security"**, click **"Download Full Site JSON Backup"** to save a copy of all your memories to your computer anytime!
